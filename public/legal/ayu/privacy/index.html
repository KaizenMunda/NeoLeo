Privacy Policy

Effective 1 January 2026 · Version 1.0 · Ayu personal health records app

1. Who we are

Ayu (“we”, “us”) operates a personal health records app. For privacy questions or grievances under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), contact: privacy@ayu.app

2. Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, profile photo (via Google sign-in)
  • Health records you upload: prescriptions, lab reports, bills, notes
  • Profile: date of birth, phone, blood type, conditions, allergies
  • ABHA Health ID (if linked): ABHA address and profile from ABDM
  • Technical: device permissions, app settings, audit logs of your actions

3. Why we process data

  • Provide the service (store, organise, search your records)
  • AI extraction — only with your explicit consent per upload session
  • Share links you create — only what you select, with expiry
  • Legal compliance and security (audit logs, fraud prevention)

4. Legal basis (GDPR) / Consent (DPDP)

For users in the EU/UK: processing is based on contract (providing the app), consent (AI processing, research sharing), and legitimate interest (security).

For users in India: we rely on consent for personal data processing, with additional safeguards for health-related data.

5. Processors & cross-border transfers

We use Supabase (database and file storage), Google (authentication; on-device OCR via ML Kit on mobile), and Anthropic (AI document extraction via our server). Cross-border transfers use appropriate safeguards where required.

6. Retention

  • Records you upload: kept until you delete them or your account
  • ABDM consent-fetched records: deleted automatically when consent expires
  • Share links: expire per your chosen duration; revocable anytime
  • Audit logs: retained for security/compliance, deleted with your account

7. Your rights

You may:

  • Access and export your data (Privacy & security → Data export)
  • Correct profile and record information in the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw AI processing or research-sharing consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (EU) or the Data Protection Board of India (DPDP)

8. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), row-level security on databases, private storage buckets for medical documents, and server-side handling of sensitive integrations (ABDM). Report issues to privacy@ayu.app.

9. Changes

We will update this notice for material changes and indicate the effective date. Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
